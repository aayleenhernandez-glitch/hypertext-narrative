FUNCTIONAL BLINDNESS.

The apartment is still quiet, except for the faint humming of the refrigerator.

You stand in front of a rice cooker, trying to prepare breakfast before leaving for the day. The buttons are covered in unfamiliar words, and a warning label sits beside the power cord.

You recognize a few letters, but not enough to understand what they are telling you. You read the instructions again, hoping something will make sense.

Your stomach growls. The clock keeps ticking.

Something as simple as making breakfast has become another decision you are afraid to get wrong.
